02Format implications for markets

Format elementMarket consequence
GSL groups (double elim, 4 teams)No team is eliminated by one Bo3 loss — there's always an elimination-match rebound path. Underdogs in the 0-1 bracket often get over-priced after a slow opener; conversely, 2-0 group winners aren't locks (they skip straight to playoffs but meet another group winner).
All-Bo3 event, Bo5 finalLonger series favor the better team (more maps = less variance). Map-handicap and total-map markets behave differently than in Bo1-heavy Swiss events; the Bo5 Grand Final is the highest-skill-correlation match of the week.
Single-elimination playoffsFour teams (the two group winners + runners-up) — one bad map pool draw ends a title run. Bracket position matters: group winners are re-seeded against runners-up in the semis.
Third-place decider playedDistinct 3rd-place market exists — and "motivation asymmetry" is real: the loser of each semifinal plays a consolation Bo3 with a $50k prize-money gap on the line.
Seven-map pool with new CacheValve added Cache to Active Duty on July 8, 2026 — barely three months of top-level sample by TWC. Teams' Cache samples are small and noisy; vetoes will reveal who did the homework. A genuine upset vector.
LAN (studio) environmentAll matches offline at BLAST Studios. Historically, some online-era over-performers regress on LAN and vice versa; sample this roster-by-roster, not in aggregate.
VRS points before Singapore MajorTWC is one of the last points events before PGL Major Singapore invites (Nov 25–Dec 13). Mid-table teams (9z, BetBoom, PARIVISION, VP) have extra seeding incentive — a motivation edge in placement markets.

03CS2 market mechanics — quick primer

Markets you'll see

  • Outright / tournament winner — typically priced after the group draw.
  • Match winner (Bo3) — plus map handicap (e.g., -1.5 maps) and total maps (2 or 3).
  • Map winner / round handicap / round totals per map (MR12: regulation max 24 rounds, typical totals 21.5/22.5/24.5; OT adds MR3 sets).
  • Correct score (2-0 / 2-1), first map winner, and player props (kills above/below, MVP).

CS2-specific edges

  • The veto is half the bet — now with 3-month evidence. Verified June 24–Sept 24 win rates: Legacy are 100% on Inferno, Aurora 100% on Nuke, BetBoom 100% on Dust2 (and played zero Inferno), FURIA 0% on Anubis, Aurora 0% on Ancient, PARIVISION 0% on Cache, Falcons played zero Cache. Full table: Stats & Form → map-pool profiles. Teams' perma-bans and pick preferences shape the map set before a shot is fired. Verified 2026 examples: FURIA's historical perma-ban Ancient; Falcons long banned Overpass (now removed from the pool entirely) and leaned Mirage/Dust2/Anubis. EGW preview data
  • Economy is cyclical: loss bonuses and force-buys create round-streakiness — one eco loss can swing 3 rounds; MR12 compresses comebacks vs the old MR15.
  • Rating ≠ star power: HLTV Rating 3.0 (introduced in August 2025) uses economy adjustment and Round Swing; check ADR, opening duels and clutches per role, not one number.
  • Schedule/venue: five-day studio LAN — no crowd-scale pressure of arena finals except the last days; short turnarounds punish deep tactical prep less than raw aim.

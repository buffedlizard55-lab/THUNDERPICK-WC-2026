This project's core rule: nothing gets published without a checkable source, and discrepancies get flagged — not smoothed over. This page holds the full ledger: entries 1–20 from the first research pass and entries 21–40 from the second pass (same day, deeper coverage) and entries 41–60 from the third pass (Sept 24: rulebook, seeding, pre-event form, schedule clash, corrections). 01Verification methodology

1 · Source hierarchy

Official first (Thunderpick releases, Valve's VRS GitHub, ESL press), then community-of-record (HLTV, Liquipedia — which itself cites primary links), then reputable specialist press. Betting-affiliate content is used only for odds/preview facts, never results.

2 · Cross-checking

Every roster change was matched to at least one HLTV news item or an official club announcement; rankings were pulled from the ranking pages/snapshots themselves, not from articles quoting them.

3 · Discrepancy handling

Where sources disagreed (prize pool, venue name, team ranks), we kept the official figure and logged the conflict in Irregularities — no silent corrections.

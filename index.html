03Format & prize money

How the Finals are played

  • Group stage: two double-elimination (GSL) groups of four; all matches Bo3; top two per group advance.
  • Playoffs: single-elimination bracket; all matches Bo3 except the Bo5 Grand Final.
  • Third-place decider is played (a real medal + prize-money difference).
  • Group draw was still TBD as of Sept 24, 2026 (Liquipedia/HLTV). Seeding uses the September VRS (official page); Valve's Sept 7 snapshot implies Falcons, Legacy, FURIA, 9z, Aurora, BetBoom, PARIVISION, VP inferred order.

05Map pool — and why Cache matters

Active Duty pool (July 8, 2026 →)

Ancient · Anubis · Cache · Dust II · Inferno · Mirage · Nuke — the seven maps available in TWC 2026 vetoes.

  • Jan 22, 2026: Anubis returned to Active Duty; Train removed.
  • July 8, 2026: Valve added Cache to Active Duty (Season 5) and removed Overpass. This is a fresh pool change, so TWC will have a smaller Cache sample than established maps.
SourcesVRedux timelineCryptoBriefing

Why it matters here

Cache has been in the pro pool for barely three months by the time of the Finals. Pro-level utility knowledge on Cache is comparatively thin, which compresses the gap between prepared teams (who grind new lineups) and improvisers — a genuine upset vector in vetoes. IEM Cologne still used the prior pool; by TWC, Cache will have less top-level history than the six established maps.

FURIA's captain announced on the IEM Rio stage (Apr 17, 2026) that he retires at the end of 2026 — "247 more days". TWC Malta is one of the final trophies he can chase, at the event he won in 2025.
